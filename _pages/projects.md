# DeepLife projects 2026

During the team until the Hackathon, you will be working on a project you will select within a list of 6 projects:

1. Project 01: Prediction of Cryptic Binding Sites with Conformational Ensemble Generation and P2Rank (D. Hoksza)
2. Project 02: Prediction of Cryptic Binding Sites Using Protein Language Models (D. Hoksza)
3. Project 03: Extending Deep Generative Markov State Models: Non-Markovian and Continuous Dynamics (G. Sergeant-Perthuis)
4. Project 04: SE(3)-Equivariant Diffusion Models for Protein Structure
Generation (G. Sergeant-Perthuis)

During the Workshop, teams from different universities who have worked on the same project will work together to compare their results and prepare a common poster presentation (check the [Workshop page](../meeting.html) for more details)
